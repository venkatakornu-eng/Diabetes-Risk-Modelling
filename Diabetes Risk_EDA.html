MA7442 Assessment 1 - Task 1

AIM :

  • The aim of this study is to Examining the socioeconomic, behavioral, and demographic characteristics linked to diabetes prevalence in the study population as well as identifying important predictors that might influence variation in diabetes status are the goals of this research.

Objectives :

  • To examine the distribution of diabetes status within the dataset.

  • To explore the relationships between diabetes and demographic variables like gender and age.

  • To evaluate the connection between biological risk factors, specifically BMI, and diabetes.

  • To explore into the relationship between the prevalence of diabetes and lifestyle choices (such as diet and alcohol use).

  • To explore the relationship between diabetes risk and socioeconomic factors, such as income level.

  • To employ multivariable modeling to identify the variables that, after adjusting for confounding variables, independently predict diabetes. (Task 2)

Overview

The dataset chosen for this research contains healthcare statistics and lifestyle survey information from the US as well as diabetic state.

  • It contains 253,680 rows and 22 columns with zero missing values.

  • It takes into account factors like age, body mass index (BMI), alcohol and fruit intake, income and diabetes.

  • Most of the values in feature classified as 0’s and 1’s which means no and yes.
